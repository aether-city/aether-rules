              Laws of the City

              Server Rules

              Every resident of AETHER CITY must follow these rules.
              Those who hold a job must also follow the Job Rules.

              1. Article I

                Be courteous

                Abusive language, discriminatory remarks and stalking toward other students or residents are forbidden.

                When trouble arises, first try to resolve it with words.

              2. Article II

                Do not abuse magic

                Using attack magic without a reason (RDM) and spamming spells while ignoring roleplay are forbidden.

                Use magic to enrich the story.

                Last winter, three were driven out of the city for this.— The Librarian

              3. Article III

                Do not bring in knowledge from outside the world

                Bringing information your character could not know, such as things learned from streams or voice chat, into the story (metagaming) is forbidden.

                Voices on the other end of a call never reach this city. Remember that.— Head Prefect

              4. Article IV

                Do not exploit bugs

                If you find a bug, do not exploit it. Report it to the staff promptly.

                Got through the wall? Write the report first.— Master of the Magic Workshop

              The rules exist to protect the story.
              Respect one another, and let us weave this world together.

              Only those who read this book to the end will find the archive's door open.

              Charters of the Guilds

              Job Rules

              Rules that those who hold a job follow while on duty.

              * Draft — subject to change

              1. Common

                All jobs

                All jobs
                1. Always go on duty when working, and go off duty when you leave.
                2. Do not use the authority, tools or funds of your job for personal purposes.
                3. Follow your coworkers and superiors, and any workplace rules.
                4. Even if you hold several jobs, you may only be on duty for one at a time.

              2. Order

                Prefects & Police

                PrefectsPolice
                1. Restraint and stun spells may only be used by Prefects while on duty. Never use them off duty.
                2. Before restraining or arresting someone, tell them the reason through roleplay.
                3. Do not leave a restrained person waiting; process them promptly (about 30 minutes at most).
                4. Respond with a number of people suited to the incident. Do not show up in excessive numbers.
                5. Do not keep seized items or money for yourself.
                6. Do not use your authority to settle personal disputes.

              3. Medical

                Magic Infirmary & EMS

                Magic InfirmaryEMS
                1. Treat patients neutrally. Never refuse treatment because of someone's affiliation or crimes.
                2. Do not enter an ongoing fight. Wait until the area is confirmed safe.
                3. Do not take part in crimes, or pass on information learned while treating or transporting patients.
                4. Do not sell or hand out medical tools or medicine while off duty.
                Friend or foe, the blood is the same color.— Old infirmary doctor

              4. Justice

                Magic Court & Lawyers

                Magic CourtHonorary Office (Judge)Magic Law OfficeLaw Office
                1. Deliver verdicts fairly, and show the parties the grounds for them.
                2. Lawyers must not reveal what clients told them without permission.
                3. Trials must not go against the Server Rules.

              5. Press

                Magic Newspaper & Reporters

                Magic NewspaperReporter
                1. Write only about events within the story. Never attack the players themselves.
                2. Do not publish information learned from streams or voice chat (meta information).
                3. Articles are published only after the editor-in-chief has reviewed them.

              6. Workshop

                Magic Workshop, Wand Workshop & Mechanics

                Magic WorkshopWand WorkshopLS CustomsBeeker's GarageBenny's
                1. Charge the prices set by the shop. Do not demand unfair fees.
                2. Repairs and modifications are done inside the workshop. Go out only when requested.
                3. Do not use vehicles or items left with you without the owner's permission.

              7. Transport

                Broom Taxi, Delivery & Towing

                Broom TaxiBroom BusFlying Carpet DeliveryLevitation TowingTaxiBus DriverTruck DriverTow Truck
                1. Do not take passengers anywhere other than the destination they asked for.
                2. Only tow abandoned vehicles. Never tow a vehicle that is in use.
                3. Do not carry passengers into restricted areas such as barriers or the night curfew zone.

              8. Trade

                Restaurants, Shops & Herb Garden

                CaféBurger ShopMagic Food StallHot Dog StandMagic Herb GardenWineryBroom ShopCar Dealership
                1. Sell goods at the prices set by the shop.
                2. Do not use the shop's stock or ingredients as your own while off duty.
                3. Advertise and call out to customers without disturbing others.

              9. Estate

                Magic Real Estate & Real Estate

                Magic Real EstateReal Estate
                1. Never sell the same property to more than one person.
                2. Clearly explain the terms of a contract (price, period, etc.) beforehand.
                3. Do not make or hand out keys to someone's home without the owner's permission.

                Forging a Persona

                Character Guide

                Creating the other you who lives in this city. It does not need to be perfect from the start — let it grow through the story.

                * Draft — subject to change

                1. Part I

                  Name

                  1. Use a first and last name that fits a person living in this world.
                  2. Do not use the names of real people or famous fictional characters.
                  3. Avoid vulgar or joke names.
                  ○ Good
                  • Alicia Grey
                  • Kyle Blackwood
                  × Avoid
                  • The same name as a celebrity or fictional character
                  • A first name only, or only symbols and numbers

                2. Part II

                  Age and appearance

                  1. Characters must be 18 years or older.
                  2. Keep the character's appearance consistent with their age.

                3. Part III

                  Backstory

                  1. Decide, even briefly, four things: where they come from, why they came to the city, their personality and their goal.
                  2. Avoid overpowered settings such as "a legendary archmage" or "cannot die". They lead to power gaming.
                  3. Reveal your backstory gradually through roleplay. There is no need to tell a stranger everything at once.
                  ○ Good
                  • Born into a family of apothecaries in a harbor town, came to the city looking for a missing sister. Shy, but can't stop talking about herbs.
                  × Avoid
                  • An immortal royal who has mastered every kind of magic and has never lost to anyone.
                  A story is better when secrets are revealed little by little.— Tavern bard

                4. Part IV

                  Your relationship with magic

                  1. Characters begin their story before awakening to magic. Magic is something gained through events in the city.
                  2. Your house is decided by the Sorting ceremony. You do not need to choose it in your backstory.
                  3. Only use magic your character has actually learned in the game.

                5. Part V

                  Multiple characters

                  1. Do not transfer money, items or information between your own characters.
                  2. Do not use what one character learned as if another character knew it (metagaming).
                  3. Do not have your characters belong to the same group or work together.

                A name is the first spell a person ever casts.

                Book of Precedents

                Precedents

                What happens if…? Common situations and examples of how they are judged.

                * Draft — subject to change

                1. Case 1

                  Right after going down, told friends the attacker's location over voice chat

                  ×Violation

                  A downed character has no way to contact friends. Passing information through voice chat outside the game is metagaming.

                  Related rules: Article III

                2. Case 2

                  Suddenly took down someone with attack magic during an argument

                  ×Violation

                  It was only an exchange of words, and no reason for an attack was shown through roleplay. This is RDM.

                  Related rules: Article II

                3. Case 3

                  During a robbery, stated demands and then threatened with magic

                  ○Allowed

                  The goal and demands were communicated, and magic was used within the flow of roleplay. This is an acceptable use that moves the story forward.

                  Related rules: Article II

                  If you're going to threaten someone, at least prepare your lines.— A nameless resident

                4. Case 4

                  Found a bug that lets you pass through walls and told the staff

                  ○Allowed

                  Reporting without exploiting is the right response. Including steps to reproduce helps the staff fix it faster.

                  Related rules: Article IV

                5. Case 5

                  An off-duty Prefect used restraint magic in a personal dispute

                  ×Violation

                  Restraint magic may only be used on duty. Using authority to settle personal disputes is also forbidden.

                  Related rules: OrderCommon

                6. Case 6

                  An infirmary worker refused to treat a member of a rival group

                  ×Violation

                  Treatment is given neutrally, regardless of affiliation. Even if the characters have history, treatment comes first while on duty.

                  Related rules: Medical

                Questions & Answers

                FAQ

                Questions the staff are often asked. For anything not covered here, use the question button on any card.

                * Draft — subject to change

                1. Q1

                  How do I join?

                  Join our Discord and apply by following the steps in the guide channel. Please read through these rules before applying.

                2. Q2

                  How many characters can I make?

                  As many as the character selection screen allows. If you have more than one, be sure to follow Character Guide, Part V.

                3. Q3

                  Can I stream or record?

                  Yes. However, you may not bring information from your stream chat or other people's streams into the game (Article III). Please be considerate of other players who appear on screen.

                4. Q4

                  What should I do if I see a rule violation?

                  Do not argue on the spot — keep roleplaying. Afterwards, report it to the Discord support channel with evidence such as a clip or screenshot.

                5. Q5

                  I lost items or money because of a bug.

                  Report the date, time and situation to the support channel, with evidence if possible. The staff will decide on compensation after checking.

                6. Q6

                  What if I want to talk about things outside the game?

                  Talk outside the game, such as on Discord. Keep OOC conversation in the game short, and only when truly necessary.

                7. Q7

                  I want to remake my character.

                  Please consult the staff in the support channel. A remade character cannot carry over the previous character's items or relationships.

                For questions without answers, ask the librarian in the corner.

                Lexicon

                Glossary

                The meaning of words used in the rules. Hover over (or tap) a dotted word in the text to see its meaning.

                1. RP

                  Roleplay

                  Becoming your character, and thinking, speaking and acting as that person. It is the heart of this server.

                2. IC / OOC

                  In character / Out of character

                  IC is what your character says and does; OOC is what you, the player, say and do. Do not bring OOC matters into your character's actions.

                3. RDM

                  Random Death Match

                  Attacking or killing another character without a roleplay reason.

                4. VDM

                  Vehicle Death Match

                  Running over or ramming other characters with a car, broom or other vehicle without a reason.

                5. Meta

                  Metagaming

                  Using information learned outside the game — from streams, voice chat or social media — as if your character knew it.

                6. PG

                  Power gaming

                  Forcing an outcome that suits you without giving others a chance to react. This includes abusing actions that would be impossible in reality.

                7. NLR

                  New Life Rule

                  A character who went down and was treated acts as if they do not remember the events just before going down.

                8. FailRP

                  Fail RP

                  Acting in a way that is unnatural for your character — for example, showing no fear at all when a weapon is pointed at you.

                9. CL

                  Combat logging

                  Leaving the game to escape an inconvenient situation such as a fight or being restrained.

                10. Duty

                  On duty

                  The state of working at your job. Clocking in at your workplace switches it on and lets you use your job's tools and authority.

                Those who know words also know spells… try casting "Lumos".

                  Banned from connecting for 1 to 7 days

                Forbidden Archive

                The Archive

                A hidden library deep within the Academy, open only to those who have read the whole Book of Rules.

                * Draft — subject to change

                This archive is sealed.
                Its doors open only to those who have read the entire Book of Rules.

                1. The Origin of Aether

                  Beneath this city runs an unseen current of power. The old books called it "Aether".

                  Long ago, people drew Aether up to light their lamps and heal the sick. In time it was traded as crystals, and today it survives as the name of the city's currency. That is why the coins in your purse sometimes feel faintly warm.

                2. The Founding of the Academy

                  The Academy was built where the currents of Aether meet most densely. Its founders believed a city with too many people of power needed a place to teach order.

                  It is said the first article of the Book of Rules is "courtesy" because that was the first thing they lost.

                3. From the Staff

                  Thank you for reading this far. The rules exist to protect your story.

                  Please keep the existence of this archive a secret from those who have not finished reading.

                  — The AETHER CITY Staff
